# Dani Moscatelli Design System: working rules

* All user-visible text must be in English.
* Every addition, change or removal in the design system (new page, component, token, menu change) must be logged as a new entry at the TOP of `src/app/components/showcase/shared/changelog.ts`. It renders in the "What's New" section on Home.
* Keep the Home "Explore" grid and the sidebar groups in `src/app/App.tsx` in sync when pages are added or removed.
